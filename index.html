The UK professional network for nurses and nursing associates who work as clinical educators

We create collaborative spaces where clinical educators can connect and find support. These include our social media communities, which are linked at the bottom of this page.

We advocate for recognition of the unique contribution clinical educators make to the quality of healthcare in the UK.

We inspire through opportunities for clinical educators to share their passion and learning in our regular newsletter and events.

Our next conference on 10 July 2026 will be hosted by Birmingham and Solihull NHS Mental Health Trust. Further information and applications will be released soon!

Join the Clinical Nurse Educator Network UK

We welcome registered nurses or nursing associates who are:

  • Working in a role that is predominantly focused on education and development of the Nursing or Nursing Associate workforce
  • Based anywhere in the United Kingdom
  • Working within any sector of health or social care (excluding higher education)
