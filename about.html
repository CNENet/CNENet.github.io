About CNENet

CNENet is an established, volunteer-run network. Our newsletter goes out to over 3000 members and our social networks include even more wonderful educators.

We are proud of the professional and geographic diversity in our membership. Our members come from every part of the nursing register including adult, mental health, learning disabilities, children’s and nursing associates. We work in every possible field, whether in the NHS, private or third sectors. We also span every corner of the British Isles across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

CNENet was established by Liz Allibone and Dr. Bill Whitehead.

The network is led and administrated by a volunteer steering committee. You can find out more about the committee below.

We are always looking for committee members, so please reach out if you'd like to contribute to the work of CNENet!

Meet the Committee

The CNENet Committee provides leadership, oversight, and strategic guidance for the network. It ensures CNENet’s activities support clinical nurse educators across the UK and facilitate professional development opportunities.
