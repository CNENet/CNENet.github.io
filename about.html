About CNENet

CNENet is an established, volunteer-run national network. Our newsletter goes out to over 3000 members and our social network channels include even more wonderful educators.

We are proud of the professional and geographic diversity in our membership. Our members come from every part of the nursing register including adult, mental health, learning disabilities, children’s and nursing associates. We work in every possible field, whether in the NHS, private or third sectors. We also span every corner of the British Isles across England, Scotland, Wales, and Northern Ireland.

CNENet was established by Liz Allibone and Dr. Bill Whitehead in 2014. Their contribution in establishing the network and creating a voice for clinical nurse educators in the UK has been invaluable in shaping the clinical nurse educator role in the UK.

The network is led and administrated by a volunteer steering committee. You can find out more about the committee below.

We are always looking for help on our working groups, ideas to enrich the experience of our membership and new committee members eager to support the network, so please reach out if you'd like to contribute to the work of CNENet at any level!
