Influencing for Clinical Education

We believe that clinical nurse educators should be involved in decisions that affect clinical education and receive recognition for the importance of their work. We work with our partners to influence strategy nationally. We also take and make opportunities to give voice to clinical nurse educators in the UK. Here is some of our work.
