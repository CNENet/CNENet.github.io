CNENet 2026 Conference

Innovating Clinical Education

Change seems to be everywhere and educators are always eager to be at the forefront of change, so this year the network will be thinking about innovations in clinical education. We want to hear about everything from large language models to art in clinical education.

This is an exciting year as we have several opportunities new to CNENet conferences, as well as some old favourites. We will have three optional workshops available for attendees in addition to the regular programme. We will have exhibitors joining us to share their wares and ideas. We will also have the return of Randomised Coffee Trials so that attendees can easily meet other clinical educators.

Dates: 10 July 2026

Host: Birmingham and Solihull NHS Mental Health Trust

Venue: The Uffculme Centre
